## Why Dual Licensing?

### Can I try before buying?

Yes. You can use AI-Automated-office under AGPL v3 for evaluation, development, and testing. You only need a commercial license when you deploy in a way that conflicts with AGPL v3.

### What happens if I don't buy a commercial license?

If you use AI-Automated-office in a way that requires source code disclosure under AGPL v3 and you do not comply, you are in violation of the license. We encourage compliance — either release your modifications under AGPL v3 or purchase a commercial license.

### Can I contribute code if I hold a commercial license?

Yes. Contributions are always welcome and are licensed under AGPL v3 as described in our [Contributing Guide](CONTRIBUTING.md). Your commercial license covers your proprietary use; contributions remain open-source.

### Is the commercial license perpetual?

Yes. Once purchased, the commercial license does not expire. However, it applies to a specific major version. Upgrading to a new major version may require a license update.
